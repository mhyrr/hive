1. **Maya Okafor:** Welcome, everyone. Quarter one in one line: forty-one clinics live, up from nineteen in January, and we survived our own mistakes, which is the theme of the next ten minutes.

2. **Ray Donnelly:** Pipeline first. Meridian Health is in their security review — fourteen clinics, which would be three times our biggest logo. Beyond that, the AU/NZ funnel is healthy: nine qualified clinics, mostly physio and chiro groups.

3. **Maya Okafor:** Before roadmap, I want to put words to something we keep saying in fragments, because how we behave when a clinic wants to leave is the whole brand.

4. **Maya Okafor:** So here it is as a company principle, and I want it written down: clinics own their data. Every record they ever entered, exportable in open formats, free, including on the way out the door.

5. **Maya Okafor:** If we ever catch ourselves designing lock-in instead of designing reasons to stay, that is the moment we have lost the plot. Retention is earned, not engineered.

6. **Nadia Hassan:** For what it's worth, that's already my favorite save-play: you can take everything and walk any time — so why walk? It disarms people completely.

7. **Jules Park:** Lowlight of the quarter, for anyone who joined the party late: the Friday migration. One innocent index change at four-thirty on a Friday afternoon, and every clinic we have couldn't take a booking for three hours. That's why the no-Friday rule exists.

8. **Maya Okafor:** And let's make that one official while we're all in the room: no schema migrations on Fridays, full stop, company policy. Dev's postmortem rule is now everyone's rule.

9. **Dev Batra:** Roadmap: one-point-three ships next week — recurring availability and the reminders overhaul. The BAA and compliance question stays parked per the March call; engineering time goes to the AU/NZ feature asks.
