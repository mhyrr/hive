Given the actual activity since the previous propose cycle ({{interval}}),
what are the smartest, most radically innovative, accretive, useful, and
compelling additions we could make to the work at this point?

Surface every proposal that clearly deserves Greg's attention. There may be
one, several, or none. Do not fill a quota.

For each proposal:

- State the addition plainly.
- Cite the signal that makes it timely.
- Explain what it compounds or makes possible.
- Name its real cost or what it would displace.
- Give the first concrete step.

If nothing clears the bar, return NO_SIGNAL.
