Given the actual activity of the last week — the nightly run artifacts and
ticket movement in the digest — what are the potentially non-obvious,
contrarian, or accretive bets we should be thinking about?

At most 3 bets; zero is a valid output. Each bet must state:

1. **The observed signal**, citing digest anchors (run artifact paths, ticket IDs).
2. **The bet itself** — one or two sentences.
3. **What it would displace or cost.**
4. **The first concrete step.**

The slop filter: if a bet could have been written without reading this week's
activity, it does not qualify. Prefer the trend across the week over any single
day's noise.
